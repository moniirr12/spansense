9. Data Retention

9.1We retain personal data for as long as your account is active and the data is needed to provide the Service, and afterwards for a reasonable period to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

9.2On request, or on termination of an account, we will delete or anonymise personal data we no longer need to retain, subject to any legal retention requirements.

10. Data Security

10.1We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk to protect personal data, including:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS) between your device and our servers;
  • passwords stored using industry-standard salted hashing, never in plain text;
  • uploaded photographs and files held in private storage, accessible only via short-lived signed links rather than public URLs;
  • role-based permissions that limit what each Authorised User can see and do within your account; and
  • server-side session management, rather than storing sensitive credentials in the browser.

10.2No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. A fuller data security policy, covering our practices in more detail, is in preparation.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

11.1The Service uses essential cookies/local storage strictly necessary to keep you signed in and to remember preferences such as dark mode. We do not currently use non-essential analytics or advertising cookies.
